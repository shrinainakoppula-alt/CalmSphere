Chess Rules & Regulations

  • White moves first. Select a piece, then click a highlighted square to move.
  • Pawns move forward one square (two on first move) and capture diagonally.
  • Knights move in an L-shape, bishops diagonally, rooks straight, queen any direction, king one square.
  • Capture by landing on an opponent's piece. Pawn promotion happens automatically on the last rank.
  • After White moves, Black will move automatically on the next turn.
  • Win by checkmate, draw by stalemate, or end the game with no legal moves.

Game Moments

Opening: develop pieces, control the center, and prepare your pawns. Middlegame: coordinate attacks, watch checks, and protect your king. Endgame: use your king actively and promote pawns.
